Progress and language are saved in this browser on this device. No account, ads, tracking, or cloud sync. Read-aloud uses a voice for the selected language from your device.

Adding something new

The included START-HERE guide explains how to upload and update the site. English lessons are in the data folder. Spanish lessons and the language controls are bundled in app.js. Ask for both languages when adding a new lesson.

Family stories and photos

Family connections were supplied by Max's family. Place photos show destinations, not family members. When published on GitHub Pages, these family connections and files can be read publicly.

Spanish read-aloud

Choose Español, then Escuchar on a lesson. The site requests a Spanish voice and prefers an available Latin American voice. If it cannot find one, add a Spanish voice in your device's accessibility speech settings. Switching language stops speech; active game rounds restart, while your passport, best scores, and pattern artwork are kept.
